What to Include in Your Listing Request

Give the editorial team enough information to confirm who the business serves and where it belongs. Include the trading name, website, main city or nationwide coverage, the most relevant category, a plain-language service summary, and your preferred contact details. If you want a logo or image considered, provide an approved, clear asset that the business has permission to use.

What Happens After You Submit

  1. Fit review: we check location, category relevance, service clarity, and whether the request suits the current directory structure.
  2. Information check: public-facing details may be checked against the business website or other material you provide.
  3. Editorial decision: TopRated may edit promotional wording, choose a more accurate category, request clarification, publish the listing, or decline the request.
  4. Future corrections: once published, factual updates can be sent through the documented correction process.

Submission Does Not Guarantee Rank

A listing request is an invitation for review, not a guarantee of publication, a particular position, or favourable editorial language. Commercial enquiries and editorial ranking are handled separately. Featured or promotional visibility is labelled where applicable.

Before submitting, read the business listing guidelines. For more detail about ranking and commercial treatment, see the review and ranking methodology and advertising and listing policy. To report an inaccurate existing listing, use the corrections process instead.
