Choose your music first, not just the street

For a specific gig or themed dance night, start with San Fran, Valhalla, or Meow and buy tickets through the venue's event listing where needed. San Fran's calendar mixes touring shows with themed dance events; Valhalla's programme ranges from heavy music to electronic and rave nights; Meow is the event-led option for concerts and special performances.

Choose a social dance-floor night

El Barrio is the clearest choice for Latin music, cocktails, food, DJs, and a Friday-Saturday 4am finish. The Establishment suits groups who want to combine food, karaoke, bands, or familiar DJ sets on Courtenay Place. S&M's is a more lounge-led Cuba Street option with events in its Basement and Dance space.

Choose a show-led or queer nightlife venue

Ivy Bar & Cabaret is the specialist pick for drag, cabaret, and queer nightlife. It is open Wednesday to Saturday from 8pm; check its social channels or event listing for the programme, entry details, and any ticket requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are the best nightclubs in Wellington?

For a late Courtenay Place dance-floor night, look at The Establishment. Cuba Street is stronger for San Fran, Ivy Bar, and S&M's, while Vivian and Edward Streets add Valhalla and Meow to the wider Te Aro circuit. El Barrio is on Dixon Street, a short walk from Courtenay Place.

Where can I find live music and club nights in Wellington?

San Fran, Valhalla, and Meow are the strongest event-calendar venues in this guide. San Fran mixes live shows with themed dance nights, Valhalla covers heavy music and electronic events, and Meow hosts ticketed concerts and one-off performances. Check the specific event rather than assuming the same music every night.

Which Wellington nightlife venues stay open latest?

El Barrio publishes Friday and Saturday hours until 4am, and The Establishment says it holds a licence until 4am. S&M's publishes Friday and Saturday hours until 3am. Event venues such as San Fran, Valhalla, and Meow can have different finishes depending on the programme.

Do I need tickets for Wellington nightclubs?

For touring artists, special DJs, drag shows, and many late-night events, buying a ticket in advance is sensible. San Fran, Valhalla, Meow, and Ivy Bar all run programme-led events. For regular bar nights, venues may offer walk-ins, but entry rules and capacity can change.

What should I wear to a Wellington nightclub?

Check the venue or event listing first. A live gig, themed dance night, drag show, and Courtenay Place late bar can have different expectations. Wear something comfortable for the event, bring appropriate ID, and follow any conditions of entry published by the venue.
