How to Choose Panel Beaters in Wellington

Start with fit, not the first name in the list. Panel beaters may handle insurance repairs, accident damage, paint matching, chassis alignment, rust, dent removal, and vehicle restoration. Write down the result you need, your timing, location, constraints, and the questions that would change your decision. That makes it easier to compare businesses on the same basis.

Wellington's hills, weather exposure, access constraints, and older building stock can affect many property and mobile services, so local experience and clear scoping matter. Shortlist two or three plausible options, check their current information, and ask for comparable answers before choosing. A strong provider should explain what is included, what is uncertain, and what you need to do next without relying on vague claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does panel beating cost in Wellington?

Minor dent repairs start from $150–$300, bumper repairs from $400–$900, and major collision work from $2,000–$10,000+. Most Wellington panel beaters offer free assessments and work directly with insurers to simplify the claims process.

How long does panel beating take in Wellington?

Minor cosmetic repairs take 1–3 days, while significant collision damage can take 1–4 weeks depending on parts availability. Top Wellington panel beaters provide courtesy vehicles during longer repairs.

Do Wellington panel beaters work with all insurance companies?

Yes, the best Wellington panel beaters are approved repairers for all major NZ insurers including IAG (State, AMI), Tower, AA Insurance, and Vero. They handle the entire claims process on your behalf.
