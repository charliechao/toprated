Questions to Ask Before Hiring

  • Is this a self-service wash, staffed hand wash, mobile groom, or professional detailing package?
  • Exactly which interior and exterior tasks are included in the advertised price?
  • Are there additional charges for SUVs, vans, heavy soiling, pet hair, stains, tar, or travel?
  • Does a mobile service bring its own water and power, or must I provide access?
  • How long will the service take, and do I need to leave the vehicle?
  • Is machine washing suitable for my paint protection film, ceramic coating, or soft-top roof?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a car wash cost in Wellington?

Current published examples start at $2 for 1.5 minutes at SmartWash and from $6 at Porirua Car Wash. Dynamic Car Grooming lists a $45 hand wash and $65 hand wash with vacuum. SelectValet mobile services start at $49, while premium detailing can start around $139 for exterior work or $279 for a full detail.

Where are the self-service car washes in Wellington?

SmartWash operates self-service sites at 35 Constable Street in Newtown and 28 Main Road in Tawa. Porirua Car Wash at Porirua Mega Centre is another self-service option and is advertised as open 24 hours.

Which Wellington car washes come to my home or workplace?

SelectValet is fully mobile and brings its own equipment. Spotless Detailing provides mobile service when water and power are available nearby, while Clean Car Collective offers mobile service alongside its Te Aro studio and CBD drop-off options.

What is the difference between a car wash and car detailing?

A car wash mainly removes surface dirt from the exterior and may include a quick vacuum. Detailing is more thorough and can include stain extraction, upholstery shampooing, pet-hair removal, paint decontamination, polishing, scratch reduction, and protective coatings.

Do Wellington mobile detailers need access to water and power?

It depends on the provider. SelectValet says it brings what it needs, including power and shelter. Spotless Detailing asks for water and power within 20 metres for mobile work, with a studio or drop-off option when that is not available. Confirm site requirements when booking.
