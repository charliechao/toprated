How to Choose a Cleaning Service in Wellington

In Wellington, a cleaning service is often judged as much by reliability and communication as by the final polish. Wind, rain, apartment access, and tight parking can all affect how smoothly a cleaning job runs.

1. Choose the Right Type of Cleaner

A domestic cleaner, a commercial office team, and a move-out cleaning crew solve different problems. Start with the type of job, then compare businesses that regularly work in that lane.

2. Ask About Access and Scheduling

Wellington properties can involve apartment access rules, hillside parking, or tight CBD time windows. A cleaner who already works in your part of the city may be more dependable than a team stretching across the region.

3. Get Clear on Scope

Ask whether bathrooms, kitchen detailing, linen changes, windows, oven cleaning, or supplies are included. The strongest cleaners make the scope obvious before you commit.

4. Check Staff Trust Signals

If someone will be entering your home, office, or rental property, ask about insurance, vetting, and who supervises quality. Consistency matters a lot for repeat cleaning work.

5. Compare the Service Experience, Not Just the Rate

Fast replies, easier rebooking, and clearer issue resolution can easily be worth more than a slightly cheaper hourly price.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring

  • Do you mainly handle residential cleaning, office cleaning, or end-of-tenancy jobs?
  • How do you handle apartment access, parking, and Wellington weather-related delays?
  • What exactly is included in the quote, and which extras cost more?
  • Are cleaners insured, supervised, and assigned consistently for repeat jobs?
  • How quickly can you schedule regular cleans or urgent move-out work?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a house cleaner cost in Wellington?

Residential cleaning in Wellington typically costs $35–$55 per hour per cleaner. Deep cleans and end-of-tenancy cleans are usually quoted as fixed-price packages ranging from $250–$600 depending on property size.

Do Wellington cleaners bring their own supplies?

Most professional Wellington cleaning services provide their own equipment and eco-friendly cleaning products. Some offer the option to use your preferred products. Always confirm this when booking.

How often should I book a cleaner in Wellington?

Weekly cleaning is most popular for busy Wellington professionals, with fortnightly being the most common budget-friendly option. Many services also offer one-off deep cleans for spring cleaning, pre-sale preparation, or move-in/move-out situations.
