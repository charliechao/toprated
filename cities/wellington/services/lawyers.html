How to Choose Lawyers in Wellington

Start with fit, not the first name in the list. Law firms may specialise in property, commercial work, disputes, family matters, employment, trusts, estates, immigration, or criminal law. Write down the result you need, your timing, location, constraints, and the questions that would change your decision. That makes it easier to compare businesses on the same basis.

Wellington's hills, weather exposure, access constraints, and older building stock can affect many property and mobile services, so local experience and clear scoping matter. Shortlist two or three plausible options, check their current information, and ask for comparable answers before choosing. A strong provider should explain what is included, what is uncertain, and what you need to do next without relying on vague claims.

What to Compare Before Choosing

Compare relevant expertise, strategy and communication, and fees and scope before you decide. These checks are more useful than choosing from a headline rating, a single price, or a broad promise alone.

Relevant expertiseAsk who will handle the matter and what recent experience they have with similar facts, complexity, and urgency.

Fees and scopeRequest a clear engagement letter covering estimates, retainers, disbursements, GST, exclusions, and how changes are approved.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do lawyers charge in Wellington?

Wellington lawyer fees vary: property conveyancing from $800–$1,800, employment law from $280–$480/hour, commercial work from $320–$650/hour, and family law from $250–$450/hour. Many offer free initial consultations and fixed-fee packages for routine matters.

How do I find a good lawyer in Wellington?

Look for lawyers who specialise in your specific legal area, hold current NZ Law Society practising certificates, have strong reviews, and offer transparent fee structures. Wellington's tight-knit legal community means reputation matters — ask for referrals.

Are there free legal services in Wellington?

Yes, Community Law Wellington offers free legal advice for eligible individuals. Many Wellington lawyers also offer free 30-minute initial consultations, and Citizens Advice Bureau provides basic legal guidance at no cost.
