How to Choose Real Estate Agents in Wellington

Start with fit, not the first name in the list. Real-estate agents differ in local sales record, appraisal evidence, campaign strategy, buyer database, communication, fees, and auction or negotiation skill. Write down the result you need, your timing, location, constraints, and the questions that would change your decision. That makes it easier to compare businesses on the same basis.

Wellington's hills, weather exposure, access constraints, and older building stock can affect many property and mobile services, so local experience and clear scoping matter. Shortlist two or three plausible options, check their current information, and ask for comparable answers before choosing. A strong provider should explain what is included, what is uncertain, and what you need to do next without relying on vague claims.

What to Compare Before Choosing

Compare comparable sales, campaign plan, and fees and agreement before you decide. These checks are more useful than choosing from a headline rating, a single price, or a broad promise alone.

Comparison pointWhat to check
Comparable salesAsk for recent nearby results involving a similar property type, price bracket, condition, and buyer audience.
Campaign planCompare pricing method, preparation, photography, advertising, open homes, buyer follow-up, reporting, and negotiation.
Fees and agreementReview commission, marketing costs, agency term, cancellation, expenses, disclosures, and what happens if the property does not sell.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring

  • How do you handle comparable sales, and what should I expect before I commit?
  • How do you handle campaign plan, and what should I expect before I commit?
  • How do you handle fees and agreement, and what should I expect before I commit?
  • What is included in your quote or fee, what is excluded, and what could change the final cost?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much commission do Wellington real estate agents charge?

Wellington agent commissions typically range from 2.5% to 3.95% of the sale price, with common structures being 3.95% on the first $400,000 and 2% on the balance. Always negotiate — commission rates are not fixed by law.

What's the Wellington property market like?

Wellington's market is characterised by limited supply and strong demand, particularly in popular suburbs like Mount Victoria, Thorndon, and Karori. The compact geography and earthquake-strengthening requirements create unique market dynamics. Median prices vary significantly by suburb.

What should I look for in a Wellington real estate agent?

Look for agents with proven recent sales in your target suburb, strong vendor and buyer reviews, professional marketing capability, and deep local knowledge including awareness of earthquake ratings and council requirements. Interview at least three agents.
