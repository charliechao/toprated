How to Choose Painters in Wellington

Start with fit, not the first name in the list. Painters may specialise in interiors, exteriors, roofs, commercial work, heritage surfaces, preparation, repairs, spraying, or decorative finishes. Write down the result you need, your timing, location, constraints, and the questions that would change your decision. That makes it easier to compare businesses on the same basis.

Wellington's hills, weather exposure, access constraints, and older building stock can affect many property and mobile services, so local experience and clear scoping matter. Shortlist two or three plausible options, check their current information, and ask for comparable answers before choosing. A strong provider should explain what is included, what is uncertain, and what you need to do next without relying on vague claims.

What to Compare Before Choosing

Compare surface preparation, paint system, and site management before you decide. These checks are more useful than choosing from a headline rating, a single price, or a broad promise alone.

Comparison pointWhat to check
Surface preparationAsk exactly how washing, sanding, filling, repairs, priming, masking, and lead-paint risks will be managed.
Paint systemCompare specified products, number of coats, colour process, finish level, and suitability for the surface and exposure.
Site managementConfirm access equipment, protection, cleanup, weather planning, programme, warranty, and what is excluded.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does house painting cost in Wellington?

Interior painting in Wellington costs $35–$55 per square metre, while exterior painting ranges from $45–$75 per square metre depending on access difficulty and preparation required. A full exterior repaint of an average Wellington home costs $8,000–$18,000.

How often should I repaint my house in Wellington?

Wellington's harsh weather means exterior repainting is needed every 7–10 years — more frequently for exposed, north-facing surfaces. Quality paint and thorough preparation extend the life of your paint job significantly.

What paint is best for Wellington homes?

Premium acrylic exterior paints from brands like Dulux Weathershield, Resene Sonyx, or Wattyl Solagard are recommended for Wellington conditions. These are specifically formulated to resist UV damage, salt air, and driving rain.
