How to choose an Auckland Indian restaurant

Start with the kind of meal you want. South Indian menus are a good fit when you are looking for dosa, idli, vada or vegetarian dishes. For North Indian curries, tandoor dishes and biryani, compare the menu before you go. Contemporary restaurants can be better for a celebration or a longer evening meal.

Then narrow the shortlist by suburb and format. CBD venues are useful before a show or after work; Sandringham works well for casual dining and takeaway; Parnell and Ponsonby are better suited to a sit-down meal. Check current opening hours, booking availability and dietary details directly with the restaurant.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find Indian restaurants in Auckland?

This shortlist includes options in the CBD, Sandringham, Parnell and Ponsonby. The best suburb depends on whether you want a quick casual meal, takeaway or a longer sit-down dinner.

Which Auckland Indian restaurants serve South Indian food?

Satya and Saravanaa Bhavan list South Indian dishes such as dosa, idli and vada. Check each restaurant's current menu for the latest selection and dietary information.

How much does an Indian meal cost in Auckland?

A casual main commonly starts around $18, while a sit-down dinner can cost more depending on the venue, dishes and drinks. Confirm current prices from the restaurant's own menu.

Should I book an Auckland Indian restaurant?

Booking is sensible for Friday and Saturday evenings, group meals and popular dining rooms. Contact the restaurant directly to confirm current availability.
