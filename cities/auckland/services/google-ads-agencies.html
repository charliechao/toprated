How to choose a Google Ads agency

Start with the account and measurement plan

Before debating campaign tactics, establish who owns the account, whether you have administrator access, and what counts as a worthwhile outcome. A click is not an enquiry, and an enquiry is not always a qualified job.

Match scope to the actual constraint

If the landing page, offer, and phone handling are already working well, a focused paid-media specialist may be the right fit. If those elements still need attention, an agency that can coordinate website work and measurement may be more useful.

Ask for a decision rhythm, not a promise

Good management is usually a cycle of testing, checking lead quality, refining search terms and ads, and improving the page after the click. Ask what the first 90 days will look like and what evidence will guide the next decision.

Questions Auckland businesses ask

What should I look for in a Google Ads agency?

Ask who owns the account, how conversions are defined and checked, which campaign types are included, what reporting you receive, and how landing pages will be improved alongside the campaigns.

Should I choose a specialist or a full-service agency?

A specialist can suit a business with strong existing web and tracking foundations. A broader agency can be more useful when paid search, website updates, analytics, and creative need to be coordinated.

How much Google Ads budget does a business need?

There is no useful universal figure. It depends on search demand, click costs, geographic coverage, margin, conversion rate, and the number of services you need to promote. Ask for a plan that separates media budget from agency fees and makes the testing period clear.
