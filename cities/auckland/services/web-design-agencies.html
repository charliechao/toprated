How much does a website cost in Auckland?

Compare the deliverables first, then the tax basis and ongoing obligations. A single-page website is not a cheaper version of a five-page site with product sales, content migration or custom booking logic—it is a different scope.

Use a whole-project comparison

First-year total = build + GST where excluded + required hosting, domain, licences, content and integrations. Then list the recurring annual costs separately. Ask what happens if you change provider.

Two examples show why the headline figure is insufficient: Ultimate's basic website starts at NZ$2,099 plus GST, for up to five pages. Impact's new-site offer is NZ$1,199 including GST and first-year hosting, with NZ$324 annual hosting/domain renewal thereafter. The scope and hosting conditions differ, so neither number alone establishes better value.

For any agency, ask whether copywriting, photography, migration, accessibility work, product entry, third-party subscriptions and post-launch changes are included. Where a price is unavailable or unclear, request a written quote; do not infer it from portfolio polish or agency size.

Send the same brief to every shortlisted agency

A consistent brief makes proposals comparable and exposes omissions before you sign. Start with your customers, the action they need to take and the content or systems already in place.

  1. Purpose: enquiries, appointments, online sales or information. Define how you will recognise a successful launch without promising sales outcomes.
  2. Scope: page count, products, locations, languages, forms and required integrations. Specify who supplies approved copy and images.
  3. Relevant work: ask for two live examples with similar requirements, the agency's role, completion date and a client reference where permission allows.
  4. Ownership and access: confirm domain registration, administrator access, source/content ownership, licence transfer and exit conditions in writing.
  5. Delivery: require milestones, revision allowances, content deadlines, migration/redirect responsibilities and a launch checklist.
  6. Quality checks: agree how mobile layouts, keyboard navigation, forms, payment flows, loading performance and tracking will be tested.
  7. Support: document backups, software updates, response expectations, hosting location, annual costs and charges for extra work.

Use reviews as supporting evidence

Look for recent comments about projects like yours, communication and ongoing support. Check whether reviews concern web design or another service. A large review total, an award badge or an attractive screenshot is not a substitute for a relevant project walkthrough and a clear contract.

Questions about choosing an Auckland web designer

Which is the best web design agency in Auckland?

TopRated ranks Kiwi Web Design #1 for the Auckland small-business service-site focus of this 2027 guide, based on its published scope and packages. The right choice depends on your project: compare proposals, portfolio examples and ongoing costs. TopRated credits Kiwi Web Design for this website's design.

How much does an Auckland website cost?

The reviewed offers are not like-for-like: Small Business Web Designs lists a one-page option at NZ$299 + GST, Impact a new website at NZ$1,199 including GST, Kiwi Web Design a three-page Starter from NZ$1,290 + GST, and Ultimate a basic site of up to five pages from NZ$2,099 + GST. Check the linked source, scope and recurring charges before comparing totals.

Should I choose WordPress, Shopify or a custom website?

Start with the work the site must do. Compare product management, payments and stock integrations for a store; editing, service content and enquiry handling for a service business. Ask each agency to explain its platform choice, recurring fees, security responsibilities and how you can move later. A platform name alone does not establish build quality.

Are Google reviews enough to choose a web designer?

Reviews can help identify recurring feedback about communication and support, but the total may cover services other than web design. Read recent, relevant reviews and inspect comparable live projects. This guide does not independently audit review counts or turn star ratings into a design-quality score.

Does paying for a website guarantee Google rankings or ChatGPT citations?

No. A build can include sound technical and content foundations, but inclusion or position in a search or AI-generated answer is not guaranteed. Ask what will actually be delivered and how it will be measured, rather than accepting a guaranteed ranking claim.

What should be in a website quote?

Ask for page and product limits, content responsibilities, platform, design revisions, integrations, launch checks, ownership and access, ongoing fees and exit terms. Compare the same written brief and the total first-year and later-year cost.
